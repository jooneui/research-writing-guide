## What the post says

Karpathy recommends ASD-STE100 as a prompt for explanations that are easier to read. He sometimes relaxes the request because the full specification is stringent. He also suggests diagrams, interactive web pages, and explanatory videos when text is not the easiest format to understand.

## How this guide uses it

The guide adopts clear terms, direct sentences, and explicit actions for research writing. It does not demand formal ASD-STE100 compliance, a fixed word limit, or the standard's controlled vocabulary. Technical accuracy and the publication venue's requirements take priority.
