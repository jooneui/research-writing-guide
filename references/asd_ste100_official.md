# Official ASD-STE100 reference

- **Publisher:** ASD Simplified Technical English Maintenance Group (STEMG)
- **Specification:** ASD-STE100, Issue 9 (2025-01-15)
- **Official site:** https://www.asd-ste100.org/
- **Official issue:** https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf
- **Official downloads page:** https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html

The official specification is the authority for actual ASD-STE100 compliance. This guide borrows general clarity habits only. Relevant examples from Issue 9 include using consistent technical nouns (rule 1.11), making instructions direct, and keeping sentences short. The specification also controls vocabulary and grammar in ways that can be unsuitable for a research paper.

An AI request for “80% ASD-STE100” is a style cue, not a compliance test. Verify any text that must formally comply with the current specification against the official rules and dictionary.
